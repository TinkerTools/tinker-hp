1 # List of Potential Energy functions {#list_potential}
2 
3 Various bonded and non-bonded energy terms are implemented with analytical gradients in Tinker-HP. Parameter files associated to well established force fields such as charmm22, ambers99 or amoeba can be found in the parameters directory of the repository.
4 
5 Information about coupling of the GPU version of Tinker-HP with machine-learning libraries in order to run simulations leveraging machine learning potentials can be found [here](md_Deep-HP.html).
6 
7 - **Bond Stretching**:
8  - description:
9  - standard bond-stretching wrt to an equilibrium distance, function of distances between bonded atoms.
10  - can be HARMONIC as Taylor expansion of Morse potential through the 4th power of the bond length deviation
11  - can be MORSE or Morse potential expanded to the 4th order (MORSE4)
12  - keywords:
13  - BONDTERM only to restrict the potential to bond-stretching, none to remove all bond-stretching interactions
14  - BONDTYP: type of bond-stretching interactions, can be **HARMONIC**, **MORSE** or **MORSE4**
15  - BONDUNIT: conversion factor of bond stretch energy to kcal/mole
16  - BOND-CUBIC: cubic coefficient in bond stretch potential
17  - BOND-QUARTIC: quartic coefficient in bond stretch potential
18  - files: kbond.f90, ebond.f90, ebond1.f90, ebond3.f90
19 
20 - **Angle Bending**:
21  - description:
22  - standard angle-bending wrt to an equilibrium angle, function of angles between bonded atoms.
23  - can be in-plane, or not. For in-plane, can be harmonic, linear, fourier or gaussian
24  - keywords:
25  - ANGLETERM only to restrict the potential to angle-bending, none to remove all angle-bending interactions
26  - ANGLEUNIT: conversion factor of angle bending energy to kcal/mole
27  - ANGLE-CUBIC: cubic coefficient in angle bending potential
28  - ANGLE-QUARTIC: quartic coefficient in angle bending potential
29  - ANGLE-PENTIC: pentic coefficient in angle bending potential
30  - ANGLE-SEXTIC: sextic coefficient in angle bending potential
31  - files: kangle.f90, eangle.f90, eangle1.f90, eangle3.f90

33 - **Stretch-Bending**:
34  - description:
35  - given 3 bonded atoms, coupling between the harmonic energy of the distances wrt to their equilibrium and the harmonic energy of the angle wrt to its equilibrium
36  - keywords:
37  - STRBNDTERM only to restrict the potential to strech-bend coupling, none to remove all the associated interactions
38  - STRBNDUNIT: conversion factor of stretch bending energy to kcal/mole
39  - files: kstrbnd.f90, estrbnd.f90, estrbnd1.f90, estrbnd3.f90

43  - description:
44  - standard Urey-Bradley term, potential on distances between outer atoms of bonded angles
45  - can be repulsive (exponential), quartic or harmonic with taylor expansion up to quartic
46  - keywords

48  - UREYUNIT: conversion factor of Urey-Bradley energy to kcal/mole

51  - files: kurey.f90, eurey.f90, eurey1.f90, eurey3.f90

135 - **Lennard-Jones**:
136  - description: standard 6-12 Lennard-Jones van der Waals energy
137  - keywords:
138  - VDWTERM: only to restrict the potential to van der Waals term, none to remove all the associated interactions
139  - VDWTYP: type of van der Waals interactions, can be **LENNARD-JONES** or **BUFFERED-14-7** (Halgren)
140  - RADIUSTYPE: type of radius for LJ interactions, can be **SIGMA** or **R-MIN** (default)
141  - RADIUSSIZE: size of radius for LJ interactions, can be **DIAMTER** or **RADIUS** (default)
142  - RADIUSRULE: combination rule for radius, can be **ARITHMETIC** (default), **GEOMETRIC** or **CUBIC-MEAN**
143  - EPSILONRULE: combination rule for epsilon, can be **ARITHMETIC**, **GEOMETRIC** (default), **HARMONIC**, **HHG** or **WH**
144  - VDW-12-SCALE: scaling factor for van der Waals interactions between 1-2 connected atoms (default 0)
145  - VDW-13-SCALE: scaling factor for van der Waals interactions between 1-3 connected atoms (default 0)
146  - VDW-14-SCALE: scaling factor for van der Waals interactions between 1-4 connected atoms (default 1)
147  - VDW-15-SCALE: scaling factor for van der Waals interactions between 1-5 connected atoms (default 1)
148  - VDW-CORRECTION: apply long range van der Waals correction
149  - VDW-TAPER: proportion of the cutoff after which to apply tapering for van der Waals interactions (default 0.9)
150  - VDW-CUTOFF: cutoff for van der Waals interactions (default 9 Angstroms)
151  - VDWSHORT-CUTOFF: cutoff for short range van der Waals interactions (default 7 Angstroms)
152  - files: kvdw.f90, elj.f90, elj1.f90, elj3.f90

261 - **Restraints**:
262  - description: harmonic restraints on various geometric quantities (positions of atoms, distances between atoms...). Note that a wide variety of harmonic restraints can be designed through the Colvars interface when this library is linked with Tinker-HP.
263  - keywords:
264  - RESTRAINTERM: only to restrict the potential to geometrical restrain term, none to remove all the associated interactions
265  - RESTRAIN-POSITION: positional restraints, takes an atom or a list of atoms (-a b for a list of atoms between index a and b) as an argument. Without further indication the atoms are restrained to their original position with a force constant of 100 kcal/mol/A**2
266  - RESTRAIN-BACKBONE: restrain all atoms whose name is 'CA' to their original position with a force constant of 100 kcal/mol/A**2
267  - RESTRAIN-DISTANCE: takes to atom indexes as an argument and 2 values, the first is the value of the spring constant in kcal/mol/A**2 and the other is the distances after which a flat bottom harmonic restraint starts
268  - RESTRAIN-ANGLE: takes 3 atom indexes defining an angle as an argument and 2 values, same logic as for distances
269  - RESTRAIN-TORSION: takes 4 atom indexes defining a dihedral as an argument and 2 values, same logic as for distances
270  - files: kgeom.f90, egeom.f90, egeom1.f90, egeom3.f90

272 - **Long-range electrostatics with Smooth Particle Mesh Ewald**:
273  - description: Tinker-HP only handles Periodic Boundary Conditions where electrostatics interactions are handled through Smooth Particle-Mesh Ewald. This concerns charge-charge and multipole-multipole interactions as well as polarization and dispersion with ewald summation. Long-range intercations require 3d-fft computations that are handled in parallel within the 2decomp_fft library.
274  - keywords:
275  - EWALD: use ewald summation for electrostatics (only option available to deal with electrostatics)
276  - EWALD-CUTOFF: cutoff for real space Ewald (default 7 Angstroms)
277  - EWALD-ALPHA: alpha parameter for Ewald summation, default corresponds to an error of 10d-8 for real space with the real space cutoff
278  - EWALDSHORT-CUTOFF: cutoff for short range real space Ewald (default 5 Angstroms)
279  - EWALD-BOUNDARY: type of boundary for Ewald summation, can be 'TINFOIL' (default) or 'VACUUM'
280  - PME-GRID: number of points in each axis of the 3d PME grid, chosen by default so that the density of the grid corresponds to a low error of Ewald summation with a 7 Angstroms real space cutoff
281  - PME-ORDER: order of the splines for reciprocal part of Ewald summation with SPME, default 5
282  - DEWALD: use ewald summation for dispersion
283  - DEWALD-CUTOFF: cutoff for real space ewald summation for dispersion
284  - DEWALDSHORT-CUTOFF: cutoff for short-range real space ewald summation for dispersion
285  - DEWALD-ALPHA: alpha parameter for Ewald summation for dispersion
286  - DPME-GRID: same as PME-GRID but for dispersion
287  - DPME-ORDER: same as PME-ORDER but for dispersion
288  - files: kewald.f90, pmestuff.f90, fft_mpi.f90, 2decomp_fft routines
