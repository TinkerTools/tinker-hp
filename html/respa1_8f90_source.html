13 ! "respa1" performs a single multiple time step molecular dynamics
14 ! step using the reversible reference system propagation algorithm
15 ! (r-RESPA) via a Verlet core with the potential split into fast-
16 ! intermediate and slow-evolving portions

18 ! literature references:
19 !
20 ! D. D. Humphreys, R. A. Friesner and B. J. Berne, "A Multiple-

22 ! Journal of Physical Chemistry, 98, 6885-6892 (1994)
23 !
24 ! X. Qian and T. Schlick, "Efficient Multiple-Time-Step Integrators
25 ! with Distance-Based Force Splitting for Particle-Mesh-Ewald
26 ! Molecular Dynamics Simulations", Journal of Chemical Physics,
27 ! 115, 4019-4029 (2001)
28 !
29 ! Ruhong Zhou, Edward Harder, Huafeng Xu and B. J. Berne,
30 ! "Efficient multiple time step method for use with Ewald and

32 ! J. Chem. Phys. 115, 2348-2358 (2001)
