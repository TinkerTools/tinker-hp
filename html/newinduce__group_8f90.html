deal with communications of direct dipoles

Parameters
[in]nrhs,:number of right hand side
[in]rule,:integer determining what to do:
  • 0: start reception of direct dipoles
  • 1: start sending of direct dipoles
  • 2: wait for the communications to be done
[in]mu,:induced dipoles
[in]reqrec,:array of MPI request for reception
[in]reqsend,:array of MPI request for sending
