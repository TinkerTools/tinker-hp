19 ! Most values below are taken from 2010 CODATA reference values;
20 ! available on the web from the National Institute of Standards
21 ! and Technology at http://physics.nist.gov/constants/
22 !
23 ! The conversion from calorie to Joule is the definition of the
24 ! thermochemical calorie as 1 cal = 4.1840 J from ISO 31-4 (1992)
25 !
26 ! The "coulomb" energy conversion factor is found by dimensional
27 ! analysis of Coulomb's Law, ie, by dividing the square of the
28 ! elementary charge in Coulombs by 4*pi*eps0*rij, where eps0 is
29 ! the permittivity of vacuum (the "electric constant"); note that
30 ! eps0 is typically given in F/m, equivalent to C**2/(J-m)
31 !
32 ! The approximate value used for the Debye, 3.33564 x 10-30 C-m,
33 ! is from IUPAC Compendium of Chemical Technology, 2nd Ed. (1997)
34 !
35 ! The value of "prescon" is based on definition of 1 atmosphere
36 ! as 101325 Pa set by the 10th Conference Generale des Poids et
37 ! Mesures (1954), where a Pascal (Pa) is equal to a J/m**3
38 !
39 !
