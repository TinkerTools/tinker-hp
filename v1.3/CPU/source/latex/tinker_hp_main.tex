All releases of the Tinker-\/\-H\-P code are now being performed on Github. For news, benchmarks, and additional tutorials, please visit the \href{https://tinker-hp.org/}{\tt $\ast$\-Tinker-\/\-H\-P website$\ast$} and follow us on \href{https://twitter.com/TINKERtoolsMD}{\tt $\ast$\-Twitter$\ast$}.

In addition to Git\-Hub, a G\-P\-Us container (quick install!) is available thanks to N\-V\-I\-D\-I\-A on the \href{https://ngc.nvidia.com/catalog/containers/hpc:tinkerhp}{\tt $\ast$\-N\-V\-I\-D\-I\-A N\-G\-C's website.$\ast$} 



{\bfseries Tinker-\/\-H\-P} is a {\bfseries C\-P\-Us and G\-P\-Us} based, multi-\/precision, {\bfseries M\-P\-I} massively parallel package dedicated to long {\bfseries polarizable molecular dynamics} simulations and to polarizable {\bfseries Q\-M/\-M\-M}. Tinker-\/\-H\-P is an evolution of the popular Tinker package that conserves its simplicity of use but brings new capabilities allowing performing very long molecular dynamics simulations on modern supercomputers that use thousands of cores.

The Tinker-\/\-H\-P approach offers various strategies using domain decomposition techniques for periodic boundary conditions in the framework of the $\ast$(n)log(n) Smooth Particle Mesh Ewald$\ast$.

Tinker-\/\-H\-P proposes a high-\/performance scalable computing environment for polarizable $\ast$$\ast$(A\-M\-O\-E\-B\-A, Amberpol...)$\ast$$\ast$ and classical $\ast$$\ast$(Amber, Charmm, O\-P\-L\-S...) force fields$\ast$$\ast$ giving access to large systems up to {\bfseries millions of atoms}. It can be used on supercomputers as well as on lab clusters. Tinker-\/\-H\-P supports {\bfseries Intel} ({\bfseries A\-V\-X-\/512} enhanced version) and A\-M\-D C\-P\-Us platforms as well as {\bfseries N\-V\-I\-D\-I\-A G\-P\-Us} $\ast$(G\-T\-X-\/10xx, R\-T\-X-\/20xx, 30xx, 40xx, P100, V100, A100)$\ast$. 



Tinker-\/\-H\-P is available free of charge for A\-L\-L Academic Institutions, National Laboratories, and supercomputer centers through the global \href{https://dasher.wustl.edu/tinker/downloads/license.pdf}{\tt $\ast$\-Tinker license$\ast$}. Non-\/academic entities (e.\-g., companies, for-\/profit organizations) should contact the managing universities (see \href{license-Tinker.pdf}{\tt $\ast$license$\ast$}).
